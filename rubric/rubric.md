# Assessment Rubric Template

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Developing | Needs revision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Accurate and well-supported | Mostly accurate | Some unsupported points | Major inaccuracies |
| Evidence | Traceable and relevant evidence | Adequate evidence | Limited evidence | Evidence absent/unverifiable |
| Application | Strong application to task/context | Appropriate application | Partial application | Little application |
| Reflection | Specific, evidence-linked reflection | Clear reflection | General reflection | Minimal/no reflection |

> Replace descriptors and weights only after alignment with verified CLOs and course assessment requirements.
